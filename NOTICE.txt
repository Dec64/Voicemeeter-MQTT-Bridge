Voicemeeter MQTT Bridge
Copyright (C) 2026 Richard Cornwell <rcp@techtoknow.net>

Voicemeeter MQTT Bridge is a Windows tray application that loads the VB-Audio
Voicemeeter Remote API DLL and bridges Voicemeeter Potato controls, state,
levels, routing, buses, strip gain/mute, and recorder functions to and from
MQTT. It can optionally publish MQTT discovery information for Home Assistant
and can start Voicemeeter Potato when the bridge launches.
